Patricia Wong sits on the boards of three public companies.
She has served on the board of Helix Pharmaceuticals since
2018, where she chairs the audit committee.

Patricia Wong joined the board of Aurora Logistics in 2021 and
the board of Crestview Bank in 2023. Across Helix
Pharmaceuticals, Aurora Logistics, and Crestview Bank, her
focus has been governance and financial oversight.

Patricia Wong's tenure on each board is staggered, with the
Helix Pharmaceuticals seat expected to come up for renewal in
2027.
