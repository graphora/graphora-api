US Patent US-11923456-B2 was granted on March 17, 2026 to
Aero Systems Corp. The patent, titled "Method for cooperative
drone routing", names three inventors: Kenji Tanaka, Sara
Ahmed, and Luca Rossi.

Kenji Tanaka is listed as the first-named inventor on
US-11923456-B2. Sara Ahmed and Luca Rossi are co-inventors.
All three inventors assigned their rights in US-11923456-B2 to
Aero Systems Corp.

Aero Systems Corp's patent portfolio expanded with the grant
of US-11923456-B2.
