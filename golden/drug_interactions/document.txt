of warfarin and aspirin increases bleeding risk through the

A separate interaction exists between warfarin and amoxicillin.
amoxicillin can elevate the international normalized ratio
(INR) in patients on warfarin, necessitating dose adjustment.

There is no established interaction between aspirin and
amoxicillin in the standard reference materials.
