The lemon-garlic chicken recipe is a Mediterranean classic.
The recipe calls for chicken thighs, lemon, and garlic as its
three primary ingredients.

The chicken thighs are marinated in lemon and garlic for at
least two hours before cooking. The Mediterranean tradition
emphasizes simple combinations of chicken thighs with lemon
and garlic; this recipe stays close to that lineage.

Once the chicken thighs have absorbed the lemon and garlic
flavors, the lemon-garlic chicken is roasted until golden.
