The DS-2026-CRYSTAL-001 dataset, containing 4,800 X-ray
diffraction measurements, was collected by Dr. Yumi Tanaka and
Dr. Marcus Webb.

DS-2026-CRYSTAL-001 was produced using X-ray diffraction at
the synchrotron beamline operated by the consortium.

Both Dr. Yumi Tanaka and Dr. Marcus Webb contributed equally
to DS-2026-CRYSTAL-001's collection and curation. The dataset
DS-2026-CRYSTAL-001 is now available for community use.
