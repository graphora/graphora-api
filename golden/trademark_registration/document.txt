The trademark STARFOLD (USPTO registration USPTO-6543210)
is owned by Starfold Apparel LLC. STARFOLD is registered in
International Class 25, covering clothing and apparel.

Starfold Apparel LLC filed the original application for
USPTO-6543210 in 2022. The registration of USPTO-6543210 was
granted in 2024 with first use dated 2021.

USPTO-6543210 remains active and Starfold Apparel LLC must
file the standard Section 8 declaration within five years of
the registration date to maintain USPTO-6543210 in Class 25.
