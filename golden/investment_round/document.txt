Polyhedron AI closed its Series B funding round on April 15,
2026. The Series B for Polyhedron AI, internally tracked as
Polyhedron-Series-B-2026, raised $45M.

The Polyhedron-Series-B-2026 round was co-led by Sequoia
Capital and Andreessen Horowitz. Both Sequoia Capital and
Andreessen Horowitz had also participated in Polyhedron AI's
seed round in 2023.

Polyhedron AI plans to use the proceeds of
Polyhedron-Series-B-2026 to scale its training infrastructure.
