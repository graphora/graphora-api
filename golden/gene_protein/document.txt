The BRCA1 gene encodes the breast cancer type 1
susceptibility protein. The breast cancer type 1
susceptibility protein participates in DNA double-strand
break repair.

Loss-of-function variants in BRCA1 are associated with elevated
risk of hereditary breast and ovarian cancer, attributable to
impaired DNA double-strand break repair.

The breast cancer type 1 susceptibility protein interacts with
multiple downstream effectors as part of its DNA double-strand
break repair function.
