NeurIPS 2025 accepted three papers from our group.
"Learning Causal Structure from Noisy Observations" by Hana
Suzuki was selected for an oral presentation. "Sparse
Attention for Long-Context Models", co-authored by Felipe
Costa, received a spotlight. "Robustness of Diffusion-Based
Image Generation", also by Hana Suzuki, will appear as a
poster.

All three papers — "Learning Causal Structure from Noisy
Observations", "Sparse Attention for Long-Context Models",
and "Robustness of Diffusion-Based Image Generation" — are
in the proceedings of NeurIPS 2025.

Hana Suzuki authored two of the three accepted papers.
