Voltaic Holdings is the parent company of three operating
subsidiaries: Voltaic Energy, Voltaic Storage, and Voltaic Grid.

Voltaic Energy is the group's solar-generation arm. Voltaic
Storage manages the group's battery storage portfolio. Voltaic
Grid handles transmission-and-distribution operations.

All three subsidiaries report to Voltaic Holdings's executive
committee on a quarterly basis. Voltaic Holdings does not
itself operate any generation, storage, or distribution
assets — it is a pure holding entity.
