Two carriers operate weekly service between Shanghai and
Long Beach. Pacific Star Lines runs the Shanghai-Long Beach
route on a Wednesday-departure schedule. Trident Shipping
runs the same Shanghai-Long Beach route on a Saturday-
departure schedule.

Both Pacific Star Lines and Trident Shipping use the same
deep-water terminal at Long Beach. The Shanghai origin port
hosts dedicated berths for Pacific Star Lines and Trident
Shipping.

Pacific Star Lines and Trident Shipping have alternating
sailings into Long Beach, providing twice-weekly capacity on
the Shanghai-Long Beach corridor.
