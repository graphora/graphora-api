Lighthouse Press will release Elena Vargas's debut novel
"The Patterns Beneath" (ISBN 978-1-2345-6789-0) in October
2026.

Elena Vargas spent five years writing "The Patterns
Beneath". Lighthouse Press acquired the rights to ISBN
978-1-2345-6789-0 at auction in 2024.

The advance galleys of "The Patterns Beneath" (ISBN
978-1-2345-6789-0) have already begun circulating among
booksellers. Lighthouse Press has scheduled a national tour
for Elena Vargas to accompany the release.
