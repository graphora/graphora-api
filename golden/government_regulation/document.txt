On March 16, 2026, the EPA published Rule 40 CFR Part 60 in
the Federal Register. The rule is the agency's first major
update to mercury emission standards in over a decade.

Under 40 CFR Part 60, coal-fired power generation facilities
must reduce mercury emissions by 90 percent within three
years of the effective date. The EPA's published guidance
notes that compliance assessments will run on a quarterly
cadence beginning Q3 2026.

Industry response has been mixed. Trade groups representing
coal-fired power generation have requested implementation
extensions, while the EPA has indicated that 40 CFR Part 60
will remain on its current schedule. The agency emphasized
that the rule reflects four years of stakeholder consultation.
