Atlas Capital Partners purchased 250,000 shares of GLBX from
Beacon Hill Fund at a price of $48.75 per share.

The block trade of GLBX between Atlas Capital Partners and
Beacon Hill Fund settled on a T+1 basis. Atlas Capital
Partners now holds a 4.2 percent stake in GLBX following the
purchase.

Beacon Hill Fund had held its position in GLBX since 2021 and
realized a gain on the sale to Atlas Capital Partners.
