NCT04501239 is a Phase 2 clinical trial sponsored by Sentinel
Therapeutics. NCT04501239 evaluates sentilumab in patients
with metastatic melanoma.

The Phase 2 design of NCT04501239 enrolls 240 patients across
fifteen sites. sentilumab is administered intravenously every
three weeks.

Sentinel Therapeutics expects to read out NCT04501239's primary
endpoint in late 2027.
