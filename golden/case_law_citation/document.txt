Brennan v. Adler, 540 F.3d 218 (3d Cir. 2008) was decided by
the United States Court of Appeals for the Third Circuit.
Judge Hardiman authored the majority opinion in Brennan v.
Adler, 540 F.3d 218 (3d Cir. 2008).

The Third Circuit's reasoning in Brennan v. Adler, 540 F.3d
218 (3d Cir. 2008) addressed the standard for qualified
immunity. Judge Hardiman noted in the opinion that prior
precedent in the United States Court of Appeals for the
Third Circuit had pointed in the same direction.
