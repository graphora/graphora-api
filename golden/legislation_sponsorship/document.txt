H.R. 4521, introduced by Rep. Adam Walsh, is a comprehensive
energy bill. H.R. 4521 was referred jointly to the House
Energy and Commerce Committee and the House Ways and Means
Committee.

The House Energy and Commerce Committee held its initial
markup of H.R. 4521 last week. The House Ways and Means
Committee will take up the tax provisions of H.R. 4521 next
month.

Rep. Adam Walsh's office expects floor action on H.R. 4521
in the fall session.
