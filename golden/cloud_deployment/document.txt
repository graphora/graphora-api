The search-api service is deployed to three regions:
us-east-1, eu-west-1, and ap-southeast-2. The search-prod
cluster spans all three regions.

In us-east-1, the search-api runs in a multi-AZ
configuration. The eu-west-1 deployment of search-api was
added in early 2025. ap-southeast-2 came online for search-api
in March 2026.

The search-prod cluster's control plane is replicated across
us-east-1, eu-west-1, and ap-southeast-2 with quorum-based
consensus.
