First Cascade Bank extended a $4.2M term loan to Skyline
Construction. The loan is collateralized by two pieces of
equipment: EQUIP-CRANE-7831 and EQUIP-EXCAVATOR-2204.

Both EQUIP-CRANE-7831 and EQUIP-EXCAVATOR-2204 are listed as
secured collateral on the security agreement between First
Cascade Bank and Skyline Construction.

Skyline Construction retains operational use of
EQUIP-CRANE-7831 and EQUIP-EXCAVATOR-2204 throughout the
loan term. First Cascade Bank holds a perfected security
interest until the loan to Skyline Construction is repaid in
full.
