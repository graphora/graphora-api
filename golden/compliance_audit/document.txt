Quintar Compliance completed ISO 27001:2022 audits at two
clients last quarter: Northwind Logistics and Southshore
Holdings.

Quintar Compliance's audit of Northwind Logistics surfaced
three observations against ISO 27001:2022 controls. The
parallel audit of Southshore Holdings against ISO 27001:2022
identified one observation in access management.

Both Northwind Logistics and Southshore Holdings have
remediation plans in flight, with re-audit by Quintar
Compliance scheduled for Q3.
