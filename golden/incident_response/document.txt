Incident INC-2026-0511-001 was opened at 14:23 UTC on May 11,
2026 after the payments-api began returning elevated 5xx rates.
INC-2026-0511-001 is classified SEV-2.

Initial triage by Priya Ramanathan traced the spike to the
ledger-db. Daniel Schmidt joined the response and confirmed
that ledger-db was rejecting writes due to a full transaction
log.

Priya Ramanathan and Daniel Schmidt resolved INC-2026-0511-001
within 47 minutes. The payments-api recovered after ledger-db
rotated its transaction log; INC-2026-0511-001 is now closed.
