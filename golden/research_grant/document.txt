The National Science Foundation awarded grant NSF-2401234 to
Dr. Rohan Mehta. NSF-2401234 supports the project "Foundations
of Verifiable Neural Networks" with $1.2M over three years.

Dr. Rohan Mehta serves as Principal Investigator on
NSF-2401234. The project "Foundations of Verifiable Neural
Networks" will produce two cohorts of graduate students over
the grant's term.

The National Science Foundation's funding of NSF-2401234
reflects continued interest in formal methods applied to
machine learning.
