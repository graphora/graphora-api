Dr. Reed Marshall sat for a long-form interview with The
Atlantic this month. Dr. Reed Marshall discussed two main
topics in his interview with The Atlantic.

On AI safety governance, Dr. Reed Marshall argued for a
mandatory licensing regime for frontier models. On
open-source model release, Dr. Reed Marshall took a more
permissive stance, supporting community access to mid-tier
models.

The interview with Dr. Reed Marshall is The Atlantic's
fourth in a series on AI policy. Dr. Reed Marshall agreed
to a follow-up conversation later this year.
