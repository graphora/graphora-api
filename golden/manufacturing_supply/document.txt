The Model X assembly relies on two critical inputs. The
lithium battery for the Model X is sourced from Acme
Materials, an Ohio-based cell manufacturer. The aluminum
frame is supplied by Beta Metals, headquartered in
Pittsburgh.

Quality assurance review noted that both the lithium battery
and the aluminum frame meet specification. The Model X
production line consumes these components from Acme
Materials and Beta Metals at the cadence set by the master
schedule.
