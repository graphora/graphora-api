In March 2026, Hexagon Industries completed its acquisition of
Octane Robotics. The deal, internally referenced as
HEX-OCT-2026-03, closed on March 14, 2026.

Under the terms of HEX-OCT-2026-03, Hexagon Industries assumed
operational control of Octane Robotics's three-state
manufacturing footprint. Hexagon Industries plans to integrate
Octane Robotics's autonomous-systems group into its existing
robotics division.

Octane Robotics will continue to operate under its original
brand for at least eighteen months post-closing.
